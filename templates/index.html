What do Groups A–E mean?

The dataset labels race or ethnicity as Group A, B, C, D, or E. It does not provide a mapping to named races or ethnicities, so the form keeps those labels. You cannot determine your own group from this dataset.

To try the demo, use the group listed in a sample dataset row. Do not guess a group based on your identity.
